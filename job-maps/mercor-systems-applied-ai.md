## Compensation and benefit lines as decision tests

These lines ask the candidate to verify an offer's terms, not to claim a technical skill: annual salary ($220k–$500k), separate equity, twice-yearly bonus structure, four-year equity vesting, relocation and nearby-housing bonuses, meal stipend, gym, laundry and wellness reimbursements, and health/dental/vision coverage. An offer comparison should use the written terms, vesting schedule, location, tax situation, and eligibility rules. None of those is inferred from the top of the posted range.
